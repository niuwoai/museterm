MuseTerm classic terminal palettes

Imported 2026-10-20. Original terminal foreground, background, cursor and ANSI
colors are retained. When no cursor color is specified, foreground is used.
MuseTerm derives its window, sidebar and control colors from these palettes.
This is an adaptation for MuseTerm, not an official theme-author endorsement.
